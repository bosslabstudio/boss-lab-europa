Boss Lab Europa: guides in seven languages

Boss Lab: Robot Battle Builder is a free 2026 Android game where you build a robot boss from 60+ parts, give it an AI brain, and watch it auto-battle. Fight through a 100-boss campaign across 20 districts, or publish your boss to asynchronous Arena PvP. Android only · needs a connection · in-app purchases · not a level editor, no user-generated content.
